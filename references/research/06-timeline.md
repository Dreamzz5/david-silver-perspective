## Recent State As Of 2026-08-29

- Personal site: CEO of Ineffable Intelligence, former DeepMind RL team lead, UCL professor.
- Ineffable site: mission is a superlearner that discovers knowledge from experience, driven by reinforcement learning.
- GOV.UK and WIRED: external coverage frames the company as a major UK AI effort and a challenge to human-data-centered AI.

## Open Questions

- How much of the public Ineffable mission will translate into published technical methods?
