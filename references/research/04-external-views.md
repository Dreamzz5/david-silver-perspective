## Consistent External Interpretation

Outsiders tend to agree that Silver's unusual strength is turning clean RL ideas into landmark systems. The strongest evidence is not only theoretical writing, but the project sequence:

- Atari from pixels
- AlphaGo with deep networks plus tree search
- AlphaGo Zero and AlphaZero through self-play
- AlphaStar in real-time multi-agent play
- MuZero with learned planning-relevant models
- Experience-first superlearner agenda

## Main Blind Spot

The central critique is transfer. Games and simulators provide clear actions, quick feedback, cheap reset, and measurable reward. Human society, science, medicine, governance, and open-ended software work rarely give rewards that cleanly.

## How This Affects The Skill

The Skill should be bold about experience-driven learning, but cautious about:

- reward misspecification
- ethics and multi-objective alignment
- simulation-to-reality gaps
- sample inefficiency
- interpretability
- overgeneralizing from game benchmarks
